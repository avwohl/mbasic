                    S T A R   T R E K
              ADAPTED BY L.E. COCHRAN  2/29/76

OBJECTIVE
  You command the Enterprise.  Destroy every Klingon battle
  cruiser in the galaxy before your time runs out.  The number
  of Klingons, the number of starbases and the time you have
  are all printed when the game begins.

THE GALAXY
  The galaxy is 8 by 8 quadrants, and each quadrant is 8 by 8
  sectors.  The short range sensor display shows the sectors
  of the quadrant you are in, using these symbols:

      E   the Enterprise            K   a Klingon cruiser
      B   a starbase                *   a star
      .   empty space

  Beside the display you are shown the stardate, your
  condition, quadrant, sector, energy, torpedoes remaining and
  the number of Klingons left.  Condition is GREEN normally,
  YELLOW when energy runs low, RED when Klingons are in the
  quadrant, and DOCKED when you are next to a starbase.

  The long range sensors and the galactic records show three
  digits per quadrant: Klingons, starbases and stars.  So 205
  means two Klingons, no starbase, five stars.

COMMANDS
  At the COMMAND prompt enter a number:

      1  WARP ENGINES           move the Enterprise
      2  SHORT RANGE SENSORS    look at this quadrant
      3  LONG RANGE SENSORS     look at the nine quadrants
                                around you
      4  PHASERS                fire energy at the Klingons
      5  PHOTON TORPEDOES       fire a torpedo
      6  GALACTIC RECORDS       the map you have built up

  Any other number lists the commands again.  Enter -99 to
  give up and leave the game.

COURSE
  Warp engines and torpedoes both ask for a course from 1 to
  8.9.  The directions run anticlockwise:

                        3  2  1
                         \ | /
                       4 - E - 8
                         / | \
                        5  6  7

  Fractions point between: 1.5 is halfway between 1 and 2.

WARP
  Warp factor runs from 0 to 12, and moves you that many
  sectors.  Anything over one quadrant carries you into a
  neighbouring quadrant.  Moving costs energy and time, and a
  damaged warp drive limits you to warp .2 until it is fixed.

PHASERS
  Phasers ask how many energy units to fire.  The energy is
  taken from the ship and divided among the Klingons in the
  quadrant; the further away a Klingon is, the less damage it
  takes.  You cannot fire more energy than you have.

PHOTON TORPEDOES
  A torpedo flies the course you give it until it hits
  something.  It destroys a Klingon, a star or - be careful -
  a starbase.  You start with ten and they are not replaced
  except by docking.

DAMAGE AND REPAIR
  Klingon hits and space storms damage the ship's systems, and
  a damaged system is unusable until repaired.  Repairs happen
  as time passes.  Occasionally Mr Spock finds a new repair
  technique and fixes something early.

STARBASES
  Move next to a starbase and you dock automatically: energy
  and torpedoes are restored to full, and the starbase
  protects you from Klingon fire while you are there.

WINNING
  Destroy the last Klingon and the Federation is saved.  Run
  out of time or energy and you are demoted to cabin boy.
